## Context

The installed development session is Sway/Pixman on the 568×1232 RM69A10 panel with GT9895 touch, Foot, wvkbd, a persistent top bar, and a desktop-entry launcher. The bar's physical evidence is in `docs/evidence/shell-real-touch-*`; it proves the rollback path, not the final interface. The bounded live-card owner and selected opt-in Sway 1.12/wlroots 0.20.2 composition route are documented in `the-shell-manages-apps-as-cards` and `docs/research/card-composition-architecture.md`. Their physical cost and real-finger gates remain open. `docs/research/handheld-ux/interaction-contract.md` supplies contrast, geometry, focus and recovery evidence classes; this proposal supersedes its persistent-bar placement as a final design requirement.

The proposed [surface sheet](../../../docs/design/handheld-shell/surfaces.svg), [motion storyboard](../../../docs/design/handheld-shell/motion-storyboard.svg), [route map](../../../docs/design/handheld-shell/transitions.md), and [motion research](../../../docs/design/handheld-shell/motion-research.md) are original planning artifacts, not installed captures. The [Palm Pre User Guide](https://support.bell.ca/_web/guides/User-Guides/Mobile/PalmOne/Palm-EN/palm_pre_userguide_en%28en%29.pdf) documents app→card, card→Launcher upward gestures, horizontal cards, flick and throw; it does not provide measured animation timings. [Android's current navigation guide](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9079646?hl=en) is a comparison for bottom swipe and app discovery, while [Android's developer guidance](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/touch-and-input/gestures/gesturenav) shows why edge/keyboard conflict policy matters. This shell chooses the Palm-style live deck as Home rather than an Android home grid or hold-for-recents path. None of these sources proves K230 behavior.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** A full gesture interface with one compositor-owned motion scene, live cards as Home, drawer from below, shade from above, truthful Settings, private notifications, predictable Back/keyboard behavior, and tested physical recovery. Desktop-entry icons accompany names and never leak private app identity.

**Non-Goals:** Permanent navigation rail/footer in the final session; synthetic universal Back into arbitrary apps; an Android-style second home grid; a new compositor or GPU chosen without measured reason; haptic work on the current board; battery/telephony/account services; changing the sibling's bounded live-card source/close lifecycle.

## Decisions

1. **Home is the deck, not a grid.** Bottom-edge upward movement from an app progressively shrinks its live surface into the deck. A release past the Home threshold settles in the deck; an early release returns to the app. Home invoked while already in the deck is stable. Empty Home is a calm deck canvas with a visible upward drawer affordance and no invented card. Terminal is an explicitly named recovery app in the drawer. This avoids two competing home destinations. The installed top bar remains only in a separately selectable rollback session until all gesture routes pass physical proof; it is absent from final normal composition.

2. **A second upward movement reveals installed apps.** From the deck, an upward drag begins at the bottom drawer handle and moves the drawer with the finger; release past a threshold settles it open, otherwise it returns to the deck. From an app, the first bottom gesture lands in the deck; it never skips directly into an unexpected app launch. The drawer scrolls vertically and may swipe horizontally between sections when more than one exists. App rows, eligible live card headers, and trusted app notifications resolve desktop-entry Icon through the installed icon theme with fixed sizing, lazy/bounded decode cache and a generic fallback. Names remain text. A private card and privacy-redacted notification use a neutral placeholder and reveal neither title nor app-specific icon; no icon association is retained in displayed private card state. Search remains deferred. An optional accessibility aid, deliberately opened from Help in the drawer or a settings preference, may expose large labeled route controls; it is not persistent chrome.

3. **Top-edge shade owns notifications and Settings.** A downward pull from the top reveals a nonmodal shade over the current scene. It contains a notification list and Settings entry; ordinary previews enter near its top edge and retreat into history/count without shifting or focusing the app. Settings opens within the shade's spatial path, not a separate System button. A side swipe on a history item reveals Dismiss and commits only after a clear threshold; critical ongoing events remain. The existing privacy/priority/event broker policy stays intact. A later `org.freedesktop.Notifications` adapter is optional after explicit shell/system and integrated-app sources work.

4. **Back is contextual shell navigation.** An inward swipe from a qualified side edge closes the top shell context: long-press sheet, Settings, shade, drawer, then keyboard when keyboard focus owns the touch. A Back gesture in app content is not translated to an arbitrary Escape/Alt-Left keypress. Apps differ in navigation semantics; app content keeps its own scroll/selection/edge handling outside a verified shell edge. Bottom Home remains the shell escape from an app or overlay. Help and accessibility aid explain routes, but permanent Back/Home buttons are rejected for the final interface.

5. **One touch owner wins at down and can cancel cleanly.** The compositor arbitrates bottom Home/drawer, top shade, side contextual Back, keyboard insets, live-card drag, active overlay and app content using measured hit regions and direction/distance gates. The keyboard's lower region wins against bottom navigation there; a visible affordance or tested outer region still reaches Home, and keyboard dismissal never types into the app. A second contact, focus loss, view unmap, motion reversal, or premature release cancels from the current visual position, without turning the stream into a tap. The exact regions are provisional until real-finger trials; failed conflict gates keep the existing rollback session active. A gesture cue is shown at rest/first use, then can quiet down; it never needs a full-width button dock.

6. **Use the selected Sway scene owner for cross-surface motion.** The sibling card implementation owns live source eligibility, direct drag, expand, graceful-close request/refusal and seat focus lifecycle. This successor adds a single interruptible spatial state above those events. Sway owns the live scene trees, touch arbitration, z-order and focus; launcher/shade clients submit intent and geometry, without trying to animate another app's pixels. App→deck maps one live rectangle into its card slot; deck→drawer rises from the bottom, shade→Settings descends from the top; card→app expands from its visible slot; throw tracks up and disappears only on observed exit. Current transform, target, velocity, source identity, pointer/focus owner and z-order are kept for reverse/retarget. A new gesture starts from the displayed state, not a reset keyframe. Client-only fake thumbnails, a second DRM owner, and a default new compositor are rejected because they cannot meet the live/focus contract without separate proof.

7. **Motion is measured, not copied.** Palm/Android references supply action/direction, not numeric timing. Finger tracking occurs for every accepted movement. A bounded velocity estimate settles a card to at most one adjacent slot per release initially; lists coast inside content and stop on new touch without activating a row. Reduced motion shortens settling but retains drag, scene identity, destination and feedback. Scene damage/output scheduling owns animation ticks; frame-done and output-presented are logged separately. The approximately 52.19 Hz advertised mode implies about 19.2 ms nominal intervals, not measured scanout. Declare p95/p99 input-to-present, frame interval, CPU/RSS and blank-frame budgets before board tuning; proposed review target is no unexplained blank frames and p95 input-to-present within two observed output intervals during two live apps. If Pixman misses, trim decoration and unnecessary damage first; do not replace live tracking with a static capture. A renderer/compositor change needs a separate cost decision.

8. **Visual grammar and truthful controls stay unified.** Canvas `#101822`, surface `#1B2934`, raised `#263946`, text `#F4F7F8`, secondary `#C8D7DD`, accent `#78D7CB`, warning `#FFC27A`, error `#FFB2A8`; title/card/body/meta roles 42/32/22/18 pixels, 8-pixel spacing rhythm, 24-pixel inset, source-color text contrast at least 4.5:1, and 56-pixel interactive targets at scale 1. The final design uses small top/bottom gesture cues rather than persistent rails. Settings rows expose only read/write capabilities confirmed by the installed image; failed changes preserve the last confirmed value and a contextual way out. No battery UI or vibration work is planned on this board. Fresh-home defaults come from Nix.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Bottom or side gesture conflicts with wvkbd or app content] → test physical ownership and cancellation; retain rollback bar session until the final route passes, then remove it from normal composition.
- [Gesture discovery is weak without permanent buttons] → show small drawer/shade/Home cues in context and offer a deliberately opened Help/accessibility aid; test first-use success on glass.
- [Live motion misses Pixman frame cost] → keep opt-in scene and measured rollback; reduce decorative work before any separate renderer decision.
- [Private card icon or title leaks identity] → replace both before scene presentation, then test unmap/reuse and cache behavior.
- [Shade changes app focus or leaks sensitive text] → preserve focus, redact unsolicited previews, and verify active keyboard and card drags.
- [No proven haptic actuator] → all accepted touch states have complete visual feedback; vibration is outside this change.

## Migration Plan

Land the revised planning artifacts first. Build host route/gesture and icon fixtures, then drawer, shade/Settings, notification state, and the coordinated scene on the selected opt-in Sway route after the sibling live-card gates. The installed persistent bar remains a separate rollback session, not a staged visual element of the final session. Only switch the default after a reserved board run proves bottom Home, empty deck, drawer, shade/Settings, contextual Back, keyboard escape, app navigation conflicts, real-glass motion and recovery. Keep unmet physical tasks open. Stage 1, kernel, and device tree are unaffected.
