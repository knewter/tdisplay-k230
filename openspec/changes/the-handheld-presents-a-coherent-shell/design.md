## Context

The installed system has a 568×1232 portrait RM69A10 panel and GT9895 touch; physical shell evidence is in `docs/evidence/shell-features/` and `docs/evidence/shell-real-touch-*`. The shell is Sway/Pixman with Foot, a persistent top bar, wvkbd, and a native desktop-entry launcher. The launcher gesture/metadata overview, catalog curation, recovery wording, card-composition probe, and live-card product are separately owned OpenSpec changes. `docs/research/handheld-ux/interaction-contract.md` sets the 56-pixel target, contrast, focus, and visible fallback baseline. Its proposed frames and this change are not shipped behavior.

The original visual proposal is [the SVG contact sheet](../../../docs/design/handheld-shell/surfaces.svg) with [motion storyboard](../../../docs/design/handheld-shell/motion-storyboard.svg), [transition map](../../../docs/design/handheld-shell/transitions.md), and [historical motion research](../../../docs/design/handheld-shell/motion-research.md). These are 568×1232 schematic surfaces at half scale, annotated as proposed. They prove layout intent only. Historical reference comes from the [Palm Pre User Guide](https://support.bell.ca/_web/guides/User-Guides/Mobile/PalmOne/Palm-EN/palm_pre_userguide_en%28en%29.pdf), especially card view and Quick Launch around pages 23–26 and notifications/dashboard later in the guide, and the archived [HP developer description of banner and dashboard notifications](https://www.banneret.nl/webos/documentation/all/dev-guide/mojo/dashboards-notifications.html). We borrow continuity, direct touch, and deferred notification handling; we use neither Palm assets nor its specific gesture strip, nor television webOS. These sources describe historical design, never K230 capability. The system and board evidence cited above grounds the current hardware boundary.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** A common shell grammar and motion system led by finger-following interaction; explicit route and focus ownership; settings backed by actual adapters; notifications that preserve context and privacy. Host fixtures can prove state policy and scene continuity before physical review.

**Non-Goals:** Assuming a new compositor or GPU path, duplicating the bounded core-card lifecycle, haptic/vibration integration on this board, inventing another sensor, battery UI, an OS lock screen, or making mockup pixels a shipped promise.

## Decisions

1. **Give the shell one spatial grammar with explicit navigation.** The target experience is app pixels shrinking into the same card seen in Windows, with adjacent cards maintaining position and an action dock shared by Windows and Apps. A compact nonmodal preview gives way to a deferred dashboard in System. These are the useful Palm phone webOS qualities; their pixels and gesture strip are not copied. The persistent top rail labels are exactly Apps, Windows, Keyboard, System. System opens a hub with separate Settings and Notifications entries; a pending count appears on System, and a preview itself opens its event. Each overlay has separate Back and Home footer actions. Final Home means the idempotent Apps landing; Terminal recovery is named Terminal in Apps and the recovery route. The installed Home-to-Terminal behavior remains during opt-in rollout until Apps/Terminal fallback and real-glass recovery pass together, then the modified `runtime/shell` requirement takes effect. The top rail stays through staged delivery because it is the installed, physically used entry path while the app-to-card route remains unverified. A future bottom dock is not selected now: the keyboard occupies that region and physical reach/occlusion evidence is absent. The final visual target is the integrated deck and dashboard, not merely the current bar with new colors.

2. **Use a quiet dark material, not blur.** Canvas `#101822`, surface `#1B2934`, raised `#263946`, text `#F4F7F8`, secondary `#C8D7DD`, accent `#78D7CB`, warning `#FFC27A`, error `#FFB2A8`, 2-pixel focus stroke. Typography is Nix-packaged sans/Pango roles 42/32/22/18 pixels for title/card/body/meta. Space in 8-pixel steps; 24-pixel page inset; at least 56-pixel controls. Current Sway output is 568×1232 at scale 1, so those logical target pixels currently map 1:1 to panel pixels; any later scale must preserve physical target dimensions. Keep overlaid text on opaque fills and source-color contrast at least 4.5:1. Reject wallpaper blur, translucent live glass, and gradients under body text because CPU/Pixman cost and optical contrast are unproved. Confirm actual glass contrast and target reach with camera and real finger evidence.

3. **Integrate cards through the selected composition owner.** Windows opens the existing metadata route until the card product has physical live-surface proof. The sibling `the-shell-manages-apps-as-cards` owns source eligibility, direct card drag, tap expansion, throw-close request/refusal, and focus lifecycle. This successor owns a coordinated motion layer over those states, including app→card continuity, deck travel/snap, expansion/return, and launcher/notification choreography. The selected route is opt-in pinned Sway 1.12/wlroots 0.20.2 with Pixman and sole DRM ownership (`docs/research/card-composition-architecture.md`); Sway has the view scene trees, seat operation, and presentation path needed for app pixels. The launcher and notification clients remain userspace views but exchange intent/geometry with this scene owner; they do not independently animate another app surface. A client-only compositor illusion or second DRM owner is rejected because it cannot authoritatively transform live app content or focus. A new compositor is a later measured decision only if this route cannot meet the contract.

4. **Use desktop entries as the sole app catalog source, with touch-native browsing.** Apps groups readable action labels and preserves built-ins, Help, failure copy, and keyboard viewport. Vertical content follows the finger, flicks coast within bounds, and a new touch stops motion. Horizontal swipes move between catalog sections only when sections exist; visible section markers and the adjacent sliver explain direction. Previous/Next remain optional accessibility controls, not the principal browsing design. A long press shows a cancellable context sheet after a visual hold cue; it never launches on release after a hold. `the-launcher-explains-app-actions` owns catalog curation. Search is deferred until keyboard focus, matching, and cost can be proven; the mockup shows no nonfunctional search box.

5. **Settings use explicit capability adapters.** A userspace settings view receives read state, write availability, and confirmed value from narrow system interfaces. Initial rows: network status/open existing Wi-Fi flow; display brightness only if the installed backlight interface can be read and written; keyboard/reduced-motion preferences; restart/power actions through the existing authorized system helper. Unavailable rows explain why and offer Back, never optimistic success. Nix supplies defaults for fresh homes. No battery row or battery-only boot state. Reject a broad privileged settings daemon or UI toggles without a real control path.

6. **Notifications are event records, not arbitrary popups.** A userspace broker accepts bounded source/title/body/action metadata, assigns monotonic IDs and ordinary/important/critical priority, deduplicates repeated events, and limits history (proposed 50 entries/7 days; adjust only from measured use). Preview is a non-focus-taking strip below the rail, at most one at a time; pending events queue into history and count on System. Ordinary preview expires after 5 seconds; important after 8; critical remains until acknowledged or condition clears. Only trusted system producers may assert critical. Unknown producers are ordinary and body-redacted. The first integration covers shell/system events and apps deliberately wired to the broker. A later `org.freedesktop.Notifications` adapter may accept standard desktop events while respecting the same privacy/priority policy; it is not needed for the first proof and no capture of every desktop event is claimed. History shows full detail after deliberate open; transient previews show neutral summary for sensitive events. Actions are typed open/retry/acknowledge routes, validated at invocation; dismissal is separate. Reject modal ordinary alerts, unbounded history, and executing sender-provided commands.

7. **Arbitrate touch once, then keep ownership.** On down, explicit bar targets and keyboard hit regions win there; app content owns its ordinary scroll, selection, and control touches; launcher/Settings/History own their list regions; card seat operation owns deck drags and throws. A narrow app edge can enter the deck only after the compositor proves it does not steal app gestures or keyboard touches; a deliberate directional threshold and cancellation zone are tested with real fingers. After acceptance, no preview or another client reinterprets the same stream. Long press has an initial visual cue, then a context sheet; motion beyond the hold slop, second contact, Back, or focus loss cancels without an accidental tap. Active drag, keyboard typing, confirmation, and app focus are never stolen by ordinary/important previews. Critical messages preserve Back/Home. Keyboard activation reserves its measured lower inset. Back closes history/settings/Apps top-first; Home is the explicit escape. Physical edge and focus behavior remain UNVERIFIED.

8. **Treat movement as one interruptible scene state.** All shell destinations use one coordinate space at 568×1232: an app rectangle maps continuously into its card rectangle; adjacent cards retain horizontal order; a selected card expands from its current visible transform. Launcher content enters from its Apps anchor and keeps a stable content origin; Settings/History move in from the System anchor, while a small preview enters near that anchor without shifting focused app content. A throw follows the finger and may travel toward the top, but the card disappears only after observed app exit; refusal/timeout returns from its current position to the same card slot. The scene owner stores current position/scale/opacity/z-order, target, velocity, card ID, source ownership, focus owner, and pointer owner. A new gesture, notification, source unmap, or Back retargets from the *presented/current* state; it never resets to a start keyframe. Input is committed only at a valid settled destination; no black gap, stale snapshot, private pixel leak, or focus orphan is allowed. Reduced motion shortens nonessential travel but retains direct tracking and spatial origin/destination. The older 120–180ms generic interval is withdrawn: source documents give no timing, and each transition must be tuned from measured board frame and input-to-visible traces.

## Motion and feedback contract

The timing/easing values are **provisional design parameters**, not Palm measurements or achieved K230 results. While held, the surface tracks input at each accepted event; after release, a bounded velocity estimate selects the nearest eligible destination and a critically damped settle avoids long oscillation. A slow drag returns to the original slot if it does not pass the direction/distance gate. A flick may advance at most one adjacent card or catalog section per release initially, avoiding accidental multi-page jumps on this narrow screen. Lists may coast farther, but never overscroll into controls or move indefinitely. A tap during coast stops it; only a subsequent tap activates an item. Press feedback begins on down; hold cue appears only after a measured deliberate hold interval and remains cancellable. A successful throw waits for actual close/exit; refusal produces a visible return path and labelled status.

Motion parameters are shared tokens by intent (press, release/snap, surface reveal, dismissal) so neighboring surfaces have the same acceleration and reversal feel. They are not an instruction to force one duration on every distance. The composer updates only changed scene nodes and clips each to its surface; overlay z-order is bar/critical notice > active overlay > deck > app, while the keyboard retains its reserved input region. The app content and subsurfaces stay live under Sway's scene mirror rules. Clients submit intent; the Sway scene owner resolves geometry and focus. A shell animation tick is tied to output scheduling/scene damage, with frame-done and output-presented measured separately; a client frame callback alone is not panel presentation. One in-flight transition is retargetable, and a new animation does not add a second independent timer for the same surface.

At the approximately 52.19 Hz advertised panel mode, an output interval is about 19.2 ms. Before implementation, set and record a board budget for p95/p99 input-to-visible, frame interval, missed presentations, scene-build/submit CPU, memory, and first response. The proposed review target is no unexplained blank frames and p95 input-to-present within two output intervals during a representative two-app drag; whether Pixman reaches it is **UNVERIFIED** and cannot be inferred from the mode value. Log down/move/up/cancel, scene state, damage/commit, frame-done, output-presented timestamps, and camera-visible response under the same trace ID. Test reverse/retarget, second touch, unmap, close refusal, keyboard, and notice arrival. If costs fail, first trim decorative opacity/shadow/travel and unnecessary damage; core live finger tracking and clear endpoints remain required. Any compositor/renderer replacement needs a separate source/cost decision.

The current device tree and reviewed vendor V1.0 schematic do not establish a haptic actuator or Linux control path; source and revision limits are recorded in `docs/design/handheld-shell/motion-research.md`. This change plans no vibration work on the current board. Press, hold, snap, close and refusal have complete immediate visual cues. A future board with a proven actuator can get a separate proposal.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Top rail is difficult to reach on glass] → preserve visible routes now and measure all-edge real-finger use before moving it.
- [History exposes secrets on shared glass] → hide sensitive preview bodies, bound records, and test unknown sender behavior; no lock-screen security is claimed.
- [System adapters report stale state] → display last confirmed value plus loading/stale/error status; never imply a command succeeded from tap alone.
- [Notification preview collides with app/keyboard] → suppress to history and count; replay is reachable without stealing focus.
- [Cards or coordinated motion miss measured Pixman cost] → retain the working opt-in fallback, trim decoration first, and record a separate architecture decision before changing renderer; never fake live tracking with static thumbnails.
- [Gesture conflicts with app scrolling or keyboard] → qualify compositor-owned edge regions on real glass and keep Windows/Back/Apps visible while the region is disabled.
- [No proven actuator on this board] → visual feedback remains complete; no vibration control or validation gate is added.

## Migration Plan

Land this planning change first. Implement shared tokens/route/gesture state fixtures, then settings adapters and notification broker. Integrate one coordinated scene-motion layer only after the existing card architecture/product gates, including physical live-surface and cost evidence; use the opt-in Sway package and retain its rollback route. Package defaults in Nix. Ship behind a reversible shell configuration flag; failure returns to the current Apps/Windows/Keyboard/System session. Record host, installed-image, and physical-glass evidence separately before enabling by default. Stage 1, kernel, and device tree are unaffected.
