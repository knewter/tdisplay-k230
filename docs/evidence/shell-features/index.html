K230 shell feature evidence

Ten demonstrations from the physical LILYGO T-Display-K230, each with a screenshot and video.

The eight native clips sample Wayland screencopy at roughly two frames per second and retain measured capture-start intervals. They show workflow and readability, not smoothness or frame rate. Companion camera clips record the panel at 30 fps. Each card identifies injected taps or IPC launch; these automated clips do not verify finger accuracy.
