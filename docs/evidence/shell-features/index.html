K230 shell feature evidence

Thirteen demonstrations from the physical LILYGO T-Display-K230, each with a screenshot and video or transition record.

The eight native clips sample Wayland screencopy at roughly two frames per second and retain measured capture-start intervals. They show workflow and readability, not smoothness or frame rate. Companion camera clips record the panel at 30 fps. Each card identifies injected taps or IPC launch; these automated clips do not verify finger accuracy.

U-Boot splash trial

Source-built U-Boot displays the K230 logo while held at its prompt before Linux. The subsequent Linux transition exposes a physical-panel handoff defect: the status row wraps and colors change. Logo-enabled handoff is therefore evidence under investigation, not a completed continuous-splash feature.
