# evtest: touches land where they are drawn (display/touch task 4.3)

2026-09-21. Captured on the corrected image (headroom reverted), with
the device tree declaring interrupts = <23 IRQ_TYPE_LEVEL_LOW>.

METHOD. A drag proves coordinates move; it does not prove they move the
RIGHT WAY. So a single white block was drawn at a known place in the
framebuffer and tapped, twice, at opposite corners. Predictions were
written down BEFORE reading the result. Digitizer is 1024x2400, panel is
568x1232, so panel (col,row) maps to (col*1024/568, row*2400/1232).

CORNER 1 -- block at panel rows 0..240, cols 0..240, centre (120,120).
  predicted, axes correct : x  216   y  234
  predicted, axes swapped : x  100   y  507
  OBSERVED                : x ~250   y ~208     -> matches "correct"

  The block is deliberately NOT square in normalised terms (0.42 of the
  width, 0.195 of the height), which is what makes a swap detectable
  from one corner at all.

CORNER 2 -- block at panel rows 992..1232, cols 328..568, centre (448,1112).
  predicted, axes correct   : x  807   y 2166
  predicted, X mirrored     : x  216   y 2166
  predicted, Y mirrored     : x  807   y  233
  predicted, axes swapped   : x  924   y 1892
  OBSERVED                  : x ~850   y ~2130  -> matches "correct"

CONCLUSION. Both corners match the unmirrored, unswapped mapping to
within ~4% of full scale, consistent with a fingertip not landing dead
centre. The axes are neither swapped nor mirrored.

NOTE. Reported X was observed reaching 1058 during free touching, above
the 1023 implied by touchscreen-size-x = <1024>. The controller's native
X range is therefore slightly wider than the device tree declares. This
does not affect orientation but means a consumer must clamp.
