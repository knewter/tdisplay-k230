The by-id path names the reader SLOT, not the card
---------------------------------------------------
It resolves to whatever sits in slot 1. That is still the right identifier
to flash by -- it can never wander onto /dev/sda through /dev/sdd, which are
the four 8 TB members of this host's RAID10 array, and device letters were
seen to move across a replug during this session. But it is not a guarantee
about WHICH card, which is why image/sd-layout also requires the procedure
to print the target's current contents and wait for confirmation.
