======================================================================
2026-09-22. Image /nix/store/vs5mll0vif0lnq36srm6qxgx75mgwf5m-k230-sd-image.img -- the pure
`nix build .#sdImage`, docs/evidence/stage1-from-nix.txt §6. Captured by the coordinator of

agent that wrote this header did not touch the board. The transcript below is the capture

Lines worth reading
-------------------
U-Boot's bootm relocating what blinux loaded -- the first time this has been recorded on
this board (lines 35-36):

    Loading Device Tree to 000000000a0eb000, end 000000000a0ff681 ... OK

The OpenSBI banner (lines 42-93), from the OpenSBI this flake built:

    OpenSBI v1.4
    Platform Name             : LILYGO T-Display-K230
    Firmware Base             : 0x0
    Domain0 Next Address      : 0x0000000000200000
    Domain0 Next Arg1         : 0x000000000a0eb000

Next Arg1 is 0x0a0eb000 -- exactly where bootm put the device tree -- and not 0x2200000.
That is the pass-through of docs/evidence/opensbi-fdt-passthrough.txt proven in the boot
log itself, independently of the /sys/kernel/btf/vmlinux read in stage1-from-source.txt.
'BTF mismatch' occurs zero times in the whole boot (the only 'BTF' in the file is systemd's
feature string, '-BTF', on its own banner).

What this capture does NOT contain, and why 5.2 stays unticked
---------------------------------------------------------------
The SPL banner, the `PMU Major Msg:` DDR-training lines and the `U-Boot 2022.10` banner
are not here. The CH342 console bridge is on the board and loses power with it; the host
takes about 3.4 s to re-enumerate it, and stage 1 prints its banners inside that window.
The capture's own markers (lines 4-6):

    [  10.704] --- port lost (SerialException), reopening ---
    [  13.509] --- /dev/ttyACM0 not openable (SerialException: [Errno 13] could not open port /dev/ttyACM0: [Errno 13] Permission denied: '/dev/ttyACM0'),
    [  14.112] --- port opened ---

and the transcript resumes mid-blinux, at the ext4load lines. Across a COLD boot with this
bridge those banners are unrecoverable. Task 5.2 asks for `PMU Major Msg:` and `U-Boot
2022.10` from the new binary and is not ticked on inference. That the from-source SPL and
U-Boot ran is grounded by stage1-from-source.txt: the card's slots hash to this flake's
bytes, and only that U-Boot's blinux could have produced what follows.
