The first write over ums: the card is flashed in the board, and the board boots what was written (tasks 3.5, 4.2)
====================================================================================================================
2026-09-22 09:33-09:39, solomon. Authorised by the coordinator, with this image -- the one already on the
card -- so that a failure would have cost a card-reader cycle and nothing else:

  /nix/store/8jpkdpxmhyysafqv5gk8vcimw347ikqq-k230-sd-image.img   2 210 918 400 bytes

Driven end to end by tools/ums-session.py --flash under /tmp/k230-board.lock: warm reboot, K230#,
`ums 0 mmc 1`, the gadget on usb 3-4 as /dev/disk/by-id/usb-Linux_UMS_disk_0-0:0, then the project's
own tools/flash.sh against that by-id path -- its bare-device refusal, its print-what-is-on-the-target
step and its typed confirmation all ran; the tool answered the confirmation with the last 12 characters
of the by-id path it had just discovered by snapshot. The card never left the board. The transcript is
below, verbatim.

What this grounds
-----------------
  3.5  Write rate: 12.6 MB/s, 175 s for 2 210 918 400 bytes (also in docs/uboot-ums.md §3).
  4.2  An image written over USB, with the card never leaving the board, booted to the login prompt;
       the board hashed its own stage-1 slots to the image's bytes; the panel's DSI line is there.
  Also, incidentally: the from-source SPL's banner and the full DDR PMU training sequence, which
  every-blob-is-built-from-source-or-named's task 5.2 could not capture across a cold boot -- the
  bridge loses power then; it does not on `reset`.
