# Shell visual gap audit — 24 September 2026

The [interactive 568×1232 study](../handheld-prototype/README.md) and its [surface and motion plan](README.md) describe the intended direction. I compared their deck, drawer, shade and Settings frames with the installed `aab74fb78dd7` shell's native 568×1232 deck and drawer captures supplied by the board operator on 24 September, the installed Settings **loading** capture, the [real-theme host renders](../../evidence/coherent-shell/rust-visual-themes-host/README.md), and the [paired Sway/Rust QEMU composite](../../evidence/coherent-shell/real-theme-paired-qemu/README.md). The operator's raw board captures were private `/tmp` review files at this audit, not committed physical evidence; this document does not claim a camera, touch, timing or optical pass. Host renders are isolated Rust surface pixels; QEMU composites use synthetic apps. The browser study uses illustrative app output and status values, not device telemetry.

**Assessment:** the installed shell has the core route and real theme plumbing, but does not yet *look* like the high-fidelity study. Its deck and drawer still resemble engineering scaffolding. Real Yaru icons and a real Omarchy still are visible, and the normal session has no permanent Apps/Windows rail or Back/Home footer. Those are meaningful steps, but palette adoption alone does not satisfy the [shared hierarchy and spacing requirement](../../../openspec/changes/the-handheld-presents-a-coherent-shell/specs/runtime/handheld-shell-design/spec.md).

The immediate review order is panel/viewport legibility, deck identity and drawer information layout, then shade/Settings hierarchy and visible wallpaper selection. Keep the existing host, QEMU and board evidence classes separate. A replacement visual pass should show the same live app through app→deck→drawer, all real installed entries, dark and light themes, and settled Settings/notifications on the actual panel; it should retain the opt-in rollback route until the named physical gates pass. This audit adds no new capability outside the open proposals and checks none of their tasks.
